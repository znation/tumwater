## Planned

### Refused backlog entries surface as an indigo Question, not an amber alarm (the 2026-11-15 "surface as a question" decision)

**Goal.** A refused PLANS.md/BUGS.md entry reads to the operator as a **Question to answer** — the same indigo surface an open question uses — rather than a standing amber "waits on your decision" alarm that reads as "you must act." The entry stays in its own backlog file; only its *surfacing* changes. The operator still releases or cancels it (the entry drawer's Release button and `tumwater plan|bug --release|--cancel` are unchanged); the alert stops *demanding* action, so the UI's amber warning is reserved for what genuinely must be acted on. This implements the 2026-11-15 director decision (Option A) that the user answered "Let's go with Option A, surface it as a question," recorded in PRINCIPLES.md as "Refusals surface as decisions, not alarms."

**Approach.** Re-tone and reword the one builder that raises the refused alert, mirroring the open-questions alert that already sits above it in the same file:
- `pushRefusedAlert` (src/ui/team-alert-backlog.ts): change `tone` from `"amber"` to `"indigo"` — the same tone `pushQuestionsAlert` uses in the same file, already a valid `TeamAlert.tone` and already rendered on both the dashboard banner (`gui-client-team.ts`'s `alert t-<tone>`, with `.t-indigo` in gui-styles.ts:91) and the TUI attention lines, and already counted as needs-you by the GUI's `NEEDS_YOU` set (gui-client-model.ts:136). Reword the title from `…refused entry waits…on your decision` to the questions-alert phrasing `1 refused entry needs your answer` / `N refused entries need your answer` (via the `plural` it already imports). Reword `detail` to drop the alarm clause: keep the `Refused <kind>: <title>` head (built from `entryListDetail`) plus a short ` · release it or cancel it` suffix, removing the "no role takes it until you release it (tumwater plan|bug --release <n>, or the entry's drawer) or cancel it" run-on — the Release/Cancel affordance lives in the drawer and CLI, not the banner, per the user's "don't get bogged down by the details." Keep `key: "refused"` and the existing `openBacklogAction(first)` button (label "Open backlog", `arg: "<file>:<index>"`) so the operator can open the row and reconcile. Reconciliation is the recompute: once the note is lifted, `refusedEntries` no longer returns the entry and the alert clears on the next poll — no new answer-flow is added.
- `buildBacklogAlerts`'s emission order (questions, then refused), `RefusedEntry`, `refusedEntries` (src/backlog/backlog-eligibility.ts:411), and the drawer's Release button (gui-client-drawer.ts:524) all stay as-is.

**Files touched.** src/ui/team-alert-backlog.ts (`pushRefusedAlert` only); test/team-alerts.test.ts — the `"refused entries raise a standing amber alert right after the questions alert"` test (update its tone and title/detail assertions to the indigo wording; keep its emission-order and action assertions); test/status-payload.test.ts — the `"status payload carries planHolds and bugHolds parallel to plans and bugs"` test (its `…"refused"?.title` assertion); docs/how-it-works.md — the one prose spot that names the amber refused-entries alert ("reserved for a product or design decision that is the user's to make; the refused-entries alert …"). No `docs/invariants.md` invariant binds the alert's tone or wording, and no invariant-named symbol is touched, so docs/invariants.md needs no edit.

**Out of scope.** The mechanically-stale self-heal half of the recorded principle (auto-lifting a refusal whose stated objection no longer holds, so it surfaces nowhere) is a separate, larger, riskier change and is deliberately not part of this entry: the user's 2026-11-15 answer chose Option A (surface as a Question), and the director recorded pure self-heal as "a larger, riskier auto-mutation of the backlog files." If it is pursued, it is its own entry.

**Acceptance criteria.**
- Given a PLANS.md entry whose body carries a `**Refused …**` note and no other holds, the status payload's `alerts` carries one alert `{ key: "refused", tone: "indigo", title: "1 refused entry needs your answer" }`; the dashboard renders it in the indigo row and the TUI renders it in the indigo attention line — the same tone the open-questions alert uses — not amber.
- The refused alert's `detail` still leads `Refused <kind>: <title>` and names the refused entry's title, but no longer carries the alarm run-on — it does not contain "no role takes it" and does not contain the `--release` command spelling (the Release/Cancel affordance now lives only in the drawer and the CLI). Its `actions` array is still the single "Open backlog" button targeting `"<file>:<index>"`.
- With two refused entries (one plan, one bug), the alert reads `2 refused entries need your answer` and its detail carries the second entry via `— and 1 more`; the action still targets the first (plan) entry's row.
- `tumwater status --json` on a repo holding one refused plan entry and one open question shows the two alerts in the same emission order as today (questions before refused), with the refused one now indigo.
- After the operator lifts the note (the drawer's Release, or `tumwater plan --release`), the next poll carries no `refused` alert; before the release it does.
- test/team-alerts.test.ts's refused-alert test passes with the indigo tone and the new wording, and its emission-order assertion (the refused alert still lands right after the questions alert) is unchanged. No other `TeamAlert` — the open-questions alert, the seven standing amber alerts, the failing/stuck role alerts — changes tone, title, or action.
