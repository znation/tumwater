### A post-merge rejection stops reverting: the reviewer's reasons become a fix-forward by the commit's authoring role on top of main, leaving runMainRevert to a red main (reported by the user 2026-10-24)

**Goal.** A post-merge rejection costs one follow-up fix, not a revert cycle. Today `drainPostReviews` (src/merge/merge-post-review.ts:25-44) turns a rejected entry into a revert slot item, so the rejection pays a revert commit, a full `check.gateCommand` run on that revert inside the single merge slot (src/baseline/main-revert.ts:118-121), an authoring run that re-applies the whole patch from `refs/tumwater/rejected/<role>`, and then the follow-up's own gate, merge and second reviewer run. The reviewer-as-role.md payoff — the class "a post-merge review converts from hours of serial rework into one follow-up fix" — is only reached if the reasons go to the author as a fix on top of main, where the commit already is. Design: plans/post-merge-fix-forward.md, arms C.

**Requires** the accounting plan (`PLANS.md` ## Planned, "Every post-merge rejection reaches one accounted terminal state…") landed: the forwarded hand-back must carry a round and a backstop before it becomes the normal path, or the cycle it replaces becomes an uncounted loop.

**Approach.**
- src/merge/merge-post-review.ts `drainPostReviews`: a rejected entry hands the reviewer's numbered reasons to the commit's authoring role through the forwarding path (`recordForwardedChange` + the runner routing that plan adds) and settles the entry out of `post-review.json` once the reasons are handed over — the entry exists to carry them to a fix, not to wait for a revert. The revert-slot arm, its `skipChecks` guard and the `p.revert = culprit` assignment go; the review slot's own one-at-a-time behaviour is unchanged.
- src/baseline/main-revert.ts: `MainCulprit.postReview` (:31-33), `runMainRevert`'s rejection wording (:110-112) and `reason: c.postReview ? "post-review" : "main-red"` (:134) are deleted with every caller migrated — `main_revert` then has one cause and one value, a commit that turned main red, which is what `main-guardrails.ts`'s revert rules (:204-215) and docs/invariants.md:266-270 already assume.
- src/merge/merge-drain.ts: `revertReasons`' post-review branch (:76-77), the `toReviewer`/`onFailed` routing in `runMainRevert`'s failure handler (:92) and `sendReviewToRole`'s "it could not be reverted (reason)" wording collapse into one forward text — "A review after merging rejected your commit <sha> on main. It is still on main. Fix what the reviewer found in a new change:" + the reasons — which is now the only path, not a fallback.
- Every surface that says "reverted" or "waiting to revert" for a post-merge rejection says "still on main, awaiting the follow-up" instead: the rejected-entry status label's one home (src/review/post-review-render.ts:24, shared by the TUI pane, `tumwater reviews` and the dashboard), the dashboard's own copy (src/ui/gui/gui-client-pending.ts:234), the help text (src/cli/help.ts:255) and docs/cli.md:50, `post-merge-churn`'s detail (src/ui/team-alerts.ts:872-877 — coordinate with BUGS.md's churn-entry fix, which folds its rows to base role there), docs/how-it-works.md:263-274, docs/invariants.md's INV-BT2 pin list (whose "a rejecting post-merge review reverts the commit and hands it back as a revision" test is deleted with the path), docs/cli.md's `tumwater revert` wording.

- The revert machinery itself is untouched for its other job — a red main's culprit — along with the red-main watch, the revert ceiling and bugfix's heal prompt.

**Accepted tradeoff.** The rejected commit stands on main until the follow-up lands. A behavioral rejection that breaks what a test covers is caught by the existing red-main path (culprit bisect → revert → bugfix heal); one that no test covers (BUGS.md's churn entry names `ec36ff29`, reverted by `beac3bd1e` because it "drops `gitTryStarted`'s `gitCouldNotStart` throw and breaks `pickBase`'s documented contract") stands on main, green but wrong, until the fix lands. If that measurement shows the forwarded fix standing too long or too often, the levers are plans/post-merge-fix-forward.md's arm D (the reviewer's verdict declares the cause, and behavioral findings revert) or arm F (narrow `review.afterMerge` for the noisy role) — not a silent return of this path.
