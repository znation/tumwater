**Goal.** `warnOnStreakCrossings` (src/run/run-finalize.ts, called once per finished run from `finalizeRun`) answers three separate reader questions in one body: what error/recovery-failure condition fires the error-streak warning and what it says, what quiet-kill condition fires its warning and what it says, and what cut-off condition fires its warning and what it says. Each of the three streak warnings fires exactly once at its own crossing with a distinct message and reset rule (see the per-episode comments), so lifting each into one named module-private helper lets a name answer "when does this episode warn, and what does it print" instead of one read of an interleaved `if`-ladder. This is a behavior-preserving extraction only.

**Approach.** All edits are inside `warnOnStreakCrossings`'s body in src/run/run-finalize.ts; nothing else in the file, and no other file, changes.
- Add three module-private helpers, each lifting the current `if` branch of `warnOnStreakCrossings` verbatim (its crossing condition, its `warnEventBestEffort(root, role, …)` call with the same message, and its per-episode comment): `warnOnConsecutiveErrors(root: string, role: string, state: RunnerState, outcome: RunOutcome): void` (the error/recovery-failure branch, which fires only when the outcome is an error-class result *and* `consecutiveErrors` equals `ERROR_STREAK_WARN`), `warnOnQuietKill(root: string, role: string, state: RunnerState): void` (the quiet-kill branch, which fires when `quietKillStreak` equals `QUIET_KILL_RESUME_LIMIT`), and `warnOnCutOff(root: string, role: string, state: RunnerState, outcome: RunOutcome): void` (the cut-off branch, which fires only when `outcome.cutOff === true` *and* `cutOffStreak` equals `CUT_OFF_RESUME_LIMIT`). Each keeps exactly the guard it carries today, so the crossing conditions move with their episodes. `warnEventBestEffort`, `consecutiveFailuresWarning`, `ERROR_STREAK_WARN`, `QUIET_KILL_RESUME_LIMIT` and `CUT_OFF_RESUME_LIMIT` stay where they are; the helpers call them as the branches do today.
- Rewrite `warnOnStreakCrossings` to call the three helpers in their current order — `warnOnConsecutiveErrors(root, role, state, outcome); warnOnQuietKill(root, role, state); warnOnCutOff(root, role, state, outcome);` — and keep its signature and the single call site in `finalizeRun` (line 89) unchanged.
