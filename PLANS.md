**Goal.** `cmdLesson` (src/notes-cli.ts) answers four separate reader questions in one 55-line body: what `add` reads from stdin and what id it prints, what `replace` takes and prints, what `retire` requires and prints, and what `list` reads and prints. This is a behavior-preserving extraction only.

**Approach.** All edits are inside `cmdLesson`'s body in src/notes-cli.ts; nothing else in the file, and no other file, changes.
- Add four module-private helpers, each lifting the current branch of `cmdLesson` verbatim (its arity guard, its `refuse(…)` calls, its `try/catch` and `process.stdout.write(…)` tail, and the same return code): `lessonAdd(lp: string, date: string): number` (the `sub === "add"` body), `lessonReplace(lp: string, id: string, date: string): number` (the `replace` body), `lessonRetire(lp: string, id: string): number` (the `retire` body), and `lessonList(lp: string): number` (the `list` body).
